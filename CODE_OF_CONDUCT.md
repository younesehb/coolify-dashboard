# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/younesehb/coolify-dashboard/security/advisories/new) (it is visible only to maintainers) and mark it "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.
